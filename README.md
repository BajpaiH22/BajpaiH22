### 🙋‍♀️ About me

Developer and AI engineer with **12 years in IT** across India, the Netherlands and Switzerland. I build **LLM applications, RAG assistants and AI agents (MCP)**, with guardrails, tests and cloud deployment, and I am completing an **MSc in Digital Business Administration at BFH**.

- 🔭 I’m currently working on **an AI document-analysis app that answers only with cited sources** (Python, Jinja, Docker, Google Cloud Run) and my master thesis **"Who Does Glycemic AI Work For?"**, a fairness evaluation of open glucose-prediction models on public CGM data
- 👯 I’m looking to collaborate on **fairness and evaluation of clinical AI**, **AI assistants for municipalities and the public sector**, and **open-source MCP tools**
- 🤝 I’m looking for help with **end-to-end evaluation of LLM apps** (expected answers, recall and precision, LLM-as-judge) and **open CGM / wearable datasets**
- 🌱 I’m currently learning **MLOps and LLM evaluation**, **Next.js**, and **German** (B1, telc exam in January 2027)
- 💬 Ask me about **RAG, MCP servers, Claude Code workflows, prompt guardrails, GDPR in research IT, and wearables like the Empatica E4**
- ⚡ Fun fact: **I once built a wristband app that noticed stress and suggested a walk or a bath. When I am not coding, I paint and dance to old Indian classics.**
